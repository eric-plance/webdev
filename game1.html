Tile Bomber

Dodge waves of flying enemies and use only bombs to take them out. But be careful, the tiles will explode as well. Enemies will drop hover boots, tile regens or slow motion items, and depending on the wave difficulty tiles will automatically regen every few rounds. How to play: WASD or Arrows to move Use the mouse to click on the screen, and pull it back to create force, and launch the bomb. Walk over dropped items for tile re-gens, hover boots or slow motion items. Press space to dash, dashing has a 10 second cooldown and can kill enemies you dash into.
