Welcome!

This website showcases three small games I created for game jams through the years. Each game can be played directly in your browser.

My Games

Tile Bomber

Dodge waves of flying enemies and use only bombs to take them out. But be careful, the tiles will explode as well. Enemies will drop hover boots, tile regens or slow motion items, and depending on the wave difficulty tiles will automatically regen every few rounds.

Play Tile Bomber

Pie Pets

Pie pets is an idle clicker game where you collect pies and purchase pets to help automatically collect pies over time! Collect more pies, and evolve your pets while also making sure to keep your pets fed!

Play Pie Pets

The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Curse

Darkness has fallen on Termina Village... Can Link restore the village before it's too late? Use your magic to reverse the cursed items and save princess Zelda before time runs out.
