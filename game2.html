Pie Pets

Welcome to Pie Pets! I made this game for the secret santa game jam in December of 2022. Pie pets is an idle clicker game where you collect pies and purchase pets to help automatically collect pies over time! Collect more pies, and evolve your pets while also making sure to keep your pets fed! Can you collect and evolve all the pets?
